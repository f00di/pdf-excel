# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be useful and welcoming for people with different backgrounds, skill levels, and perspectives. Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and focus on improving the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections when impact differs from intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Share reproducible details when reporting problems.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private documents or personal information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private, confidential, or legally restricted material.
- Spam or repeated off-topic disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code of conduct. Serious issues can be reported privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method listed on the GitHub repository profile.
